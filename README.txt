FAVORITES SITE WITH OPTIMIZED WEBP IMAGES

This bundle contains the full site, 29 background posters, and 24 images for the six favorite shelves. Favorite images were converted to WebP at quality 90. Large images are reduced to a maximum 1200-pixel side while preserving their original proportions and keeping all artwork visible; images already smaller than this remain at their native dimensions. This retains ample detail for titles at the on-page poster size.

INSTALL
1. Extract this ZIP into your frieren-link-site folder and allow index.html, favorites.html, style.css, and script.js to be replaced.
2. Keep images/posters and the six favorite image folders beside those files.
3. Refresh Live Server.

Each category has four images named 01.webp through 04.webp in your chosen order, with titles shown below them on the Favorites page. Source originals were not modified. See CONVERSION-REPORT.txt for dimensions and before/after sizes.
